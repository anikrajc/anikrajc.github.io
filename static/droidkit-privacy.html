DroidKit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

The short version: DroidKit does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

Who this covers

DroidKit is an Android developer toolkit published by Anik Raj C (package com.anikrajc.droidkit). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with data.

Information collected

None. DroidKit contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK, and no telemetry of any kind. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or any other personal identifier, and it has no server of its own to send anything to.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it is useful across launches:

  • Your deep link execution history, including any entries you star
  • Your preferences, such as the selected target app and toggle states

This is written to Android's private app storage (SharedPreferences), which is readable only by DroidKit. It is never uploaded anywhere. You can erase it at any time using Clear history inside the app, or by uninstalling DroidKit or clearing its storage in Android Settings.

Permissions and why they are needed

DroidKit requests the following permissions purely to perform its on-device functions. Data read through them is shown to you and is never transmitted.

PermissionWhy
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Lists your installed apps so you can pick which one should handle a deep link, and shows which apps match a given URI.
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Draws the optional floating bubble launcher over other apps, so you can re-run a deep link while testing. Optional and off by default.
WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS Applies developer toggles such as layout bounds and animation scales. Android only grants this manually via adb.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
ACCESS_WIFI_STATE		 Reads network and Wi-Fi status for the network inspector. Read-only.
INTERNET Required by Android so the links and intents you launch can open.

Third-party services

Pre-release builds of DroidKit are distributed to testers through Firebase App Distribution, a Google service. If you install a pre-release build as an invited tester, Google processes your email address and basic device information in order to deliver that build to you. That processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not by this one. This applies only to build delivery — the app itself contains no Firebase data-collection SDKs.

Links you open

When you execute a deep link or URL, Android hands it to another app or browser of your choosing. What that app does with it is covered by its own privacy policy, not this one.

Children

DroidKit is a developer utility and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If DroidKit's data practices ever change — for example if crash reporting is added in a future release — this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
